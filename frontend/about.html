WELCOME TO T.B. JAYAH ZAHIRA COLLEGE

T.B. JAYAH ZAHIRA COLLEGE, COLOMBO 02, IS A RESPECTED EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTION DEDICATED TO PROVIDING QUALITY EDUCATION WHILE NURTURING DISCIPLINE, LEADERSHIP, MORAL VALUES, AND ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE AMONG ITS STUDENTS.

ESTABLISHED IN 1935, THE COLLEGE HAS CONTINUOUSLY SERVED THE COMMUNITY BY PRODUCING RESPONSIBLE CITIZENS AND FUTURE LEADERS WHO CONTRIBUTE POSITIVELY TO SOCIETY.

THE COLLEGE OFFERS EDUCATION IN BOTH SINHALA AND TAMIL MEDIUMS AND PROVIDES STUDENTS WITH OPPORTUNITIES TO EXCEL IN ACADEMICS, SPORTS, LEADERSHIP, TECHNOLOGY, AND CULTURAL ACTIVITIES.
